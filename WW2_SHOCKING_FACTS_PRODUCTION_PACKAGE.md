## STEP 6 — EDITING PLAN

### Cut Rhythm

| Phase | Cut Rate | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0:00–0:20) | Every 1.2–1.8 seconds | Rapid hard cuts — pattern interrupt |
| Fact intros | Hold 3–4 seconds | Let the visual breathe when VO delivers stats |
| Stat moments | Hard cut on the number | Smash cut to a new angle exactly as the number lands |
| Ending | 6–8 second holds | Slow down completely — reward the audience |

### Transitions
- **90% Hard cuts** — no fades, no dissolves except at the very end
- **Smash cut** — used on every shocking stat delivery
- **Very slow fade to black** — only at the final frame before end card
- **No zoom transitions** — they cheapen the documentary feel

### Text Overlays
| Type | Usage | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Fact numbers | "FACT 1", "FACT 2" etc. — white, bold, centered. 0.5s hold | Clean sans-serif, slight grain |
| Key stats | "27,000,000" — large, centered, appears word by word | White on dark, high contrast |
| Emphasis phrases | "OPERATION UNTHINKABLE" — slam onto screen | Uppercase, slight shake/impact |
| Source citations | Bottom-left corner, small — grey text | Present during key claims |
| Subscribe CTA | Final 10 seconds — bottom third | Simple, not aggressive |

### Caption Strategy
- **Full captions ON** — burn in with slight typewriter delay
- Font: Clean sans-serif (Inter or Montserrat)
- Color: White with black outline — readable on any background
- Highlight key words in **yellow** (numbers, names, shocking phrases)

### Pacing Strategy — The 30-Second Rule
Every 30 seconds something must change: new fact, new visual angle, new stat on screen. If nothing new is introduced in 30 seconds, a viewer will leave.
